Two testnet policy bundles were lost to a same-name overwrite before bundle filenames became chain-aware; their signed reports remain in the transparency logs and their attestations remain on chain, where the terminal reads them.

A report is bound to the control set it was signed under. The earliest sequences were evaluated under an earlier, larger control set, and the digest of that approval ledger sits inside the signed report. The current approved set is {{fact:counts.controls_approved}} controls under ledger 0x{{fact:approval.ledger_sha256}}, signed by {{fact:approval.approver}} on {{fact:approval.signed_on}}; it applies only to sequences published after it.

A report may also name an earlier sequence it corrects. A correction supersedes that sequence without rewriting it, so the corrected report keeps the epoch it observed — which is why the highest sequence on a chain can carry an older epoch than the one before it.
