fobxx-price-history-freshness Declined as proposed: a 3-business-day window on a business-daily T-1 feed tolerates roughly two missed deliveries, and it is looser than this project's own approved precedent for the same evidence class (ustb-nav-daily-freshness uses business_days 2 with grace_period 1). Rebuild it on the SEC submissions source, which carries filingDate, rather than on the N-MFP3 document.
