Evidence authorities

{{fact:counts.evidence_sources}} issuer-published JSON endpoints stand behind every USTB verdict. Touchstone reads public Superstate disclosures; it is not a party to them.

USTB evidence sources
Source What a control may quote from it
superstate-ustb-nav-daily The daily NAV row: the per-share value and the date it is stated as of.
superstate-ustb-yield The published yield row and the date it is stated as of.
superstate-ustb-holdings The holdings disclosure and the date it is stated as of.

Every fetch is stored as a digest-addressed capture. A control quotes a byte span from a named capture rather than a value someone retyped, the report carries the evidence root over those captures, and a reader can recompute both offline from the signed bundle. How sources are audited →
