Why this is not a dashboard

One evidence set. Two questions.

Policy is where usefulness becomes explicit. The evaluator does not change; the consumer question does.

01 / compile

AI proposes a control.

The proposal is bound to the source span and compilation digest. It cannot approve itself or rewrite a threshold.

02 / evaluate

Deterministic code checks evidence.

Freshness, confirmation distance, and source roles are evaluated from retained captures, not a model's confidence.

03 / enforce

A consumer can refuse.

AssetGate is pinned to a policy root and admits only the state its consumer selected. The testnet action is refused today.
