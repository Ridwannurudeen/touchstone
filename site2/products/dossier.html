Products · Dossier

The published report

A dossier entry is one signed epoch report for one asset. Everything the engine concluded — including what it could not conclude — travels inside one file, under one Ed25519 signature. Five are published — all USTB, across testnet and mainnet — and the dossier below is sequence 1, the first.

What a report carries

The report format is touchstone.observation-report.v4; the bundle wrapping it is touchstone.verification-bundle.v4. Each field below is real and present in the published report.

Identity and sequence

The epoch id, the asset key, and a sequence number. Sequence 1 for USTB is ustb-2026-08-17, asset eip155:1:0x43415eb6ff9db7e26a15b704e7a3edce97d31c4e.

State, with a transition

The asset's reported state and how it got there. Sequence 1 is UNVERIFIABLE, transition RECONFIRMED from a previous UNVERIFIABLE as of 2026-08-17.

Every control result

Each control appears by id and content hash with its evaluation: the result, the observed value, and the row date it belongs to. A control the evidence could not answer is present as UNEVALUABLE, not absent.

Commitments

A control set root, an evidence root, the approval-ledger digest and three compilation digests bind the report to the exact inputs it was signed over. All are recomputable from the bundle alone.

Validity

A report expires. Sequence 1 is valid until 2026-08-18T23:59:59Z; after that, a consumer treating it as current would be wrong, and the report says so itself.

Limitations, inside the signature

Five limitation strings travel inside the canonical report bytes. Removing one would change the signature. They are reproduced verbatim on this site.

Corrections are named, never silent

A report can name an earlier sequence it corrects via correction_of. The earlier report stays on chain; the correction points at it. Sequence 1 corrects nothing — its correction_of is null.

A report is also bound to the control set it was signed under. Sequence 1 was evaluated under an eight-control approval ledger whose digest is inside the signed report. The project has since approved a different, five-control set; it applies only to later sequences and is never shown against sequence 1.
