# Touchstone — trademark counsel brief

**Prepared:** 2026-08-21 · **decision status:** rebrand recommended before commercial launch
· **legal status:** not assessed

This document packages verified facts for qualified trademark counsel. It is not a legal
opinion, clearance search, registrability opinion, filing instruction, or assurance that a
name is available. No registration, reservation, purchase, outreach, or filing was performed.
The underlying search record and its limitations remain in
[`BRAND-CLEARANCE.md`](BRAND-CLEARANCE.md).

## 1. Decision requested from counsel

Please provide a written opinion answering both branches:

1. Whether the owner may continue using **TOUCHSTONE** for the goods, services, audiences and
   territories below without an unacceptable likelihood-of-confusion or passing-off risk.
2. If continued use is not recommended, what clearance process and filing strategy should be
   applied to a replacement mark before the public product, repository, SDK and social account
   are renamed.

The opinion should identify the searched jurisdictions and databases, search date, search
strings, live and common-law results, relevant goods/services, assumptions, risk analysis,
recommended classes, filing owner, and any use restrictions. Anything less remains preliminary
screening rather than professional clearance.

## 2. Business recommendation pending counsel

**Recommended: plan a controlled rebrand before commercial launch.** Continue using Touchstone
only as the name of the existing public hackathon project and its historical deployments while
counsel reviews the issue and a replacement mark is properly screened. Do not expand paid use,
seek institutional contracts, file a TOUCHSTONE application, buy a replacement domain, or rename
public assets on the strength of this document.

The recommendation is driven by the combination of three verified facts, not by package-name or
domain scarcity alone:

- **Exact name plus close function.** `touchstone-verify` offers independently verifiable,
  Ed25519-signed, hash-chained disclosures and offline verification. Its current PyPI record
  identifies version 0.5.0, uploaded 2026-07-12, and links to `touchstone.cv` and the
  `Touchstone-CV` GitHub organization.
- **Exact name plus RWA category.** A Morpho governance announcement dated 2026-07-15 presents
  Touchstone as the curator of two gold-collateral lending vaults and uses the same assayer-stone
  metaphor. The post identifies live vault addresses and markets.
- **Exact federal marks in adjacent commercial fields.** The underlying report records live US
  registrations for TOUCHSTONE in risk-analysis software and mortgage-document SaaS, and
  TOUCHSTONE-formative registrations for investment and mutual-fund services. Counsel must
  determine relatedness; class numbers alone do not resolve it.

The recommendation is deliberately conservative. It does **not** say infringement exists, that a
registration would be refused, or that any third party has superior rights in every territory.

## 3. Mark and present-use facts

### Mark under review

- Word mark: **TOUCHSTONE**.
- Current presentation: plain word plus a stylised project icon; no device mark has been submitted
  for a professional image-similarity search.
- Naming rationale: the stone historically used by assayers to test metal, used here as a metaphor
  for verifying evidence before tokenized assets are admitted.
- First public-use date and first use in commerce: **not established by the repository**. Counsel
  should obtain dated specimens and the owner's account before relying on either.
- Proposed applicant/owner: **not identified in the repository**. Counsel must establish the legal
  person or entity that owns the goodwill and will control the relevant goods/services.

### Product that exists today

Repository materials establish the following present scope:

- Open-source software that compiles issuer-published evidence into cited, machine-checkable
  controls, evaluates approved policies deterministically, produces signed reports, and supports
  offline verification.
- Smart contracts on X Layer that record reports and allow a gate or admission controller to
  permit or refuse an action based on a pinned policy state.
- A public website and browser terminal at `touchstone.gudman.xyz`, a public GitHub repository,
  a TypeScript SDK, Solidity interfaces, and an X account.
- One implemented asset vertical, USTB. Wallet and AI-agent enforcement are described as intended
  integrations, not present production functionality.
- No asset issuance, custody, investment recommendation, credit rating, legal-compliance opinion,
  or claim that an asset is safe, solvent, compliant, or suitable.
- No paid service, paid customer, commercial contract, or third-party production adoption is
  established in the repository as of the preparation date.

The controlling public descriptions are `README.md`, `ROADMAP.md` §“Category and product
boundary”, `docs/LIMITATIONS.md`, and `docs/SUBMISSION-DRAFT.md`. Counsel should treat roadmap
features as intended expansion, not current trade, unless the owner supplies later evidence.

### Intended expansion that affects the search

The roadmap contemplates hosted APIs, evidence monitoring, enterprise SLAs, institutional
workflows, protocol integrations, and policy checks consumed by wallets and AI agents. Potential
customers include blockchain protocol developers, RWA issuers, custodians, wallet providers and
institutional risk teams. Those future channels materially increase proximity to financial and
risk-management brands and should be included in counsel's forward-looking analysis.

1. Exact word: `TOUCHSTONE`.

## 10. Completion gate

“Professional brand clearance” may be marked complete only when the project holds a dated written
opinion from qualified counsel that identifies the mark, owner, territories, goods/services,
searches, material results, risk conclusion and permitted-use conditions. Until then:

- `legal_status` remains `not_assessed`;

- a rebrand is the recommended business course, not an executed decision;
- no replacement name is approved merely because a domain or handle appears unused; and
- commercial launch under either the existing or a replacement name remains gated on counsel.
