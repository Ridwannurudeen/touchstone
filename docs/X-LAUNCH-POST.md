## Recommended first post

Meet Touchstone — the institutional RWA policy and control plane for X Layer.

Price and NAV feeds tell a protocol what value was reported.

Touchstone tells it whether the evidence policy required for an action currently holds — and
gives contracts an enforceable refusal when it does not.

Our bounded AI compiler turns issuer evidence into cited, machine-checkable control proposals.
Deterministic surveillance evaluates approved controls against retained evidence. Signed
policy reports are then attested on X Layer, where a gate can permit, suspend or refuse an RWA
action.

Live proof:

• 17 reports published across X Layer mainnet and testnet

• 12 reports reached `CONFIRMED`

• 8 Registry V2 attestations

• 6 permit/refuse enforcement transactions

• `AssetGateV2` and `RWAAdmissionController` live on mainnet
• One provisional NAV refused, then confirmed only after the evidence qualified

No control was weakened to manufacture a positive result.

Our goal: make every institutional RWA action on X Layer policy-aware by default.
